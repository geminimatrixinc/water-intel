# Phase 3 — First Bids & Pilots (Weeks 9–12)

> **Goal:** By end of Week 12 you've submitted at least one real bid, opened a
> sole-source pilot conversation, and have a reference path forming. This is where
> credentials + pipeline convert into actual revenue motion.

**Status:** 🔲 Not started (begins after Phase 2)
**Time budget:** ~8–12 hrs/week
**Depends on:** Phase 2 (need live tender alerts + warm outreaches in flight)

---

## Week 9 — Submit your first bid (even a learning bid)

The first bid is about building the muscle and the template, not necessarily winning.

- [ ] **Pick one live PSIB set-aside or Indigenous-eligible tender** from your alerts that
      is in scope (AI/data/environmental) — *45 min to choose*
- [ ] **Read the solicitation header** for "set-aside under PSIB" + mandatory criteria
      (pass/fail) — confirm you meet every mandatory item before investing time — *1 hr*
- [ ] **Draft the technical response** reusing capability statement + Water-Intel case
      study as evidence — *3 hrs*
- [ ] **Build a reusable bid template** from this effort (mandatory-criteria checklist,
      boilerplate sections) so bid #2 is 3× faster — *included*
- [ ] **Submit before deadline** — *30 min*

> **Done when:** One bid is submitted and a reusable bid template exists.

---

## Week 10 — Convert outreach into a sole-source pilot conversation

Sole-source under ~$40K is your fastest path to first revenue and a reference.

- [ ] **Follow up every Phase 2 outreach** that didn't reply (one-line nudge + demo link) — *1 hr*
- [ ] **Turn at least one positive reply into a 30-min call** — *scheduling + 30 min*
- [ ] **On the call, identify a small, concrete pilot** scoped under the sole-source
      threshold (e.g., ECCC source-water anomaly summary, ISC/OFNTSC drinking-water
      advisory analytics for specific communities) — *30 min*
- [ ] **Send a 1-page pilot brief** after the call (problem · what you'll deliver ·
      timeline · price under threshold) — *1.5 hrs*

> **Done when:** One pilot brief is in a buyer's hands with a price under the sole-source threshold.

---

## Week 11 — Strengthen the proof for procurement-grade buyers

Small codebase touches that disproportionately help you win federal water work.
Keep these *light* — story matters more than production-readiness at pilot stage.

- [ ] **Integrate the boil-water-advisory dataset** you already have, framed as
      "predicting/shortening long-term drinking-water advisories on First Nations" (named
      federal priority) — *2–3 hrs*
- [ ] **Document a Protected B-ready / Canadian-cloud hosting posture** (even if running
      locally today) — a 1-page security note buyers ask for — *1.5 hrs*
- [ ] **Write AIA-ready model cards** for the anomaly + driver-hints models
      (inputs, limits, human-in-the-loop) — federal AI buyers request these — *2 hrs*
- [ ] **Record a 2-minute MCP/agent demo** — almost no Indigenous supplier ships this;
      lean on it hard in conversations — *1 hr*

> **Done when:** You can hand a buyer a security note + model cards and show a live agent demo.

---

## Week 12 — Consolidate, plan the next 90 days

- [ ] **Update the outreach + bid tracker** — what advanced, what to drop — *45 min*
- [ ] **Pick the single best opportunity** from everything in flight and concentrate
      effort there — *30 min*
- [ ] **Decide vertical #2** based on which buyer conversations were warmest (environmental
      monitoring vs. case-management vs. other) — *30 min*
- [ ] **Draft Phase 4 (weeks 13–24):** standing-offer push + first reference deployment +
      stacked second grant — *1.5 hrs*
- [ ] **90-day retro:** honest assessment — credentials done? pipeline live? first revenue
      in sight? Adjust cadence — *45 min*

> **Done when:** A clear single priority is chosen and the next 90-day plan is sketched.

---

## Phase 3 exit checklist

- [ ] ≥1 bid submitted + reusable bid template built
- [ ] ≥1 sole-source pilot brief delivered to a buyer
- [ ] Security note + model cards + agent demo ready
- [ ] Vertical #2 chosen
- [ ] Phase 4 (weeks 13–24) drafted

➡️ **Success milestone for this whole track:** first signed pilot OR first grant approval.
Everything after that compounds — references unlock bigger bids, bigger bids unlock
standing offers, standing offers replace the day job.
