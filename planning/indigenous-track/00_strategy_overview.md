# Strategy Overview — Indigenous AI Growth Track

> Read this once to understand the *why*. Then work the phase files for the *what* and *when*.

---

## 1. The thesis

**Gemini Matrix Consulting Inc. is an Indigenous-owned AI/ML systems integrator.**
Water-Intel is its first vertical product, not the company. This reframe is the whole
game: it moves you from "a water-quality startup" to "the rare Indigenous supplier who
can actually ship modern AI," which is what gets you onto standing offers and into
recurring federal/provincial revenue.

**Same core stack, many verticals.** Your ingest → anomaly detection → driver hints →
risk score → API → dashboard → MCP/agent pipeline is domain-agnostic after the loader
stage. Each new vertical is a new loader + new framing, not a new company.

---

## 2. Why now (the structural opportunity)

- **Federal:** Mandatory minimum **5% of contract value** to Indigenous business, in force
  across 96 departments. They are *actively searching* the Indigenous Business Directory
  for suppliers — especially in under-served categories like AI/ML.
- **Ontario:** Aboriginal Procurement Program (set-asides via CCAB/CAMSC certification),
  Indigenous Economic Development Fund, OPS Vendor-of-Record Indigenous streams.
- **Crown corps:** Hydro One, OPG, IESO, Metrolinx all run Indigenous procurement targets
  with real IT/environmental budgets.
- **The pool is thin on tech.** Most IBD-listed firms are construction, staffing, and
  facilities. Modern AI/agent capability is scarce — your moat.

---

## 3. The three doors in (federal PSIB)

| Door | What it is | Your access |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| **Set-Aside Program** | Competition *restricted* to IBD-listed firms | ✅ Full — you're 100% Indigenous-owned |
| **Indigenous Participation Plan (IPP)** | Open bid + enforceable Indigenous-benefit commitments | ✅ Easy — you *are* the Indigenous benefit |
| **Sole-source under threshold** | Direct award up to ~$40K under PSIB, no competition | ✅ Fastest path to first revenue |

**Ontario adds a 4th door:** Aboriginal Procurement Program set-asides (needs CCAB or
CAMSC certification — separate from federal IBD).

---

## 4. Credentials map (which key opens which door)

| Credential | Unlocks | Effort |
|-----------|---------|--------|
| **IBD listing** (Indigenous Business Directory) | All federal PSIB set-asides + the 5% target | Already registered — must verify current + correct NAICS |
| **CCAB Certified Aboriginal Business (CAB)** | Ontario provincial + most crown corps | New application (Phase 1) |
| **CAMSC certification** | Some corporate/supply-chain set-asides | Optional, later |
| **ProServices / TBIPS (Indigenous supplier)** | Federal professional-services call-ups | Phase 2 application |

> **Key insight:** IBD ≠ CCAB. IBD is the *federal* door; CCAB is the *Ontario + crown-corp*
> door. You want both. They are independent processes that can run in parallel.

---

## 5. Vertical roadmap (same stack, mapped to buyers)

| # | Vertical | Buyers (5%-target depts / orgs) | Status |
|---|----------|-------------------------------|--------|
| 1 | **Source / drinking-water intelligence** | ECCC, ISC, DFO, OFNTSC, conservation authorities, First Nations | ✅ Built (Water-Intel) |
| 2 | **Traditional-territory & environmental monitoring** | CIRNAC, NRCan, ECCC, crown corps (impact-benefit tracking) | Reuses ingest + anomaly stack |
| 3 | **Agentic case-management workflows** (e.g. Jordan's Principle) | ISC, Health Canada | Reuses MCP/agent layer |
| 4 | **Indigenous language / heritage AI tooling** | Canadian Heritage, ISED | Small-model / transcription |
| 5 | **Procurement & compliance AI** (audit the 5% target, IPP tracking) | ISC, PSPC | They're buyer *and* regulator |

Lead with #1 (you have it + it hits a named federal priority). Keep #2 architecturally
"drop-in ready" — that's what makes the company fundable, not just contract-able.

---

## 6. The buyer tiers (think bigger than one community)

- **Tier 1 — Federal departments under 5% pressure:** ECCC, ISC, CIRNAC, DFO, NRCan,
  Health Canada, Shared Services Canada (big AI budget), DND (Phase 3 — green field).
  Every dept has an Indigenous Procurement Coordinator who *answers email* because their
  numbers depend on it.
- **Tier 2 — Ontario + crown corps:** Hydro One, OPG, IESO, Metrolinx, Bruce Power.
  Real budgets, published Indigenous targets, less red tape than federal.
- **Tier 3 — Indigenous-led orgs & Nations as buyers:** OFNTSC (highest fit for water),
  Chiefs of Ontario, large bands (Six Nations, Mississaugas of the Credit, Tyendinaga).
  Fastest sales cycles, fewest gates, generate the references that unlock Tiers 1–2.

**Sequence:** Land Tier 3 references → use them to win Tier 1 sole-source pilots →
graduate to Tier 1/2 standing offers.

---

## 7. Audit & integrity — do this clean

ISC runs **post-award audits** verifying ownership/control. The ArriveCan fallout made
Indigenous-procurement integrity a political priority. You are legitimately 100%
Indigenous-owned, so this is an *advantage* — but keep it airtight:

- Keep incorporation docs, shareholder register, and proof of status current and filed.
- If you ever subcontract to a non-Indigenous tech firm, **you** must retain control and
  lead the work — never become a flow-through.
- Never overstate certification status in a bid (e.g., don't claim CCAB before it's granted).

---

## 8. The honest endgame

Run this **part-time and patient.** Replace the full-time job only when you have **2–3
signed contracts or one major multi-year grant/standing offer** — not before. The order
matters: credentials → pipeline → grant → reference pilot → standing offer → go full-time.
