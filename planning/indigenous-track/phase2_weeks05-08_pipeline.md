## Week 5 — Stand up the discovery pipeline (set once, runs forever)

- [ ] **Create CanadaBuys account** + saved searches with email alerts
      (https://canadabuys.canada.ca/en) — *45 min*
- [ ] Saved-search keywords: `set-aside`, `Indigenous`, `artificial intelligence`,
      `machine learning`, `data analytics`, `anomaly detection`, `water quality`,
      `monitoring`, `forecasting` — filter by your NAICS codes — *45 min*
- [ ] **Register on MERX** (https://www.merx.com) + set Indigenous / IT category alerts — *30 min*
- [ ] **Register on Biddingo** (Ontario municipal/broader-public) — *30 min*
- [ ] **Bookmark crown-corp supplier portals:** Hydro One, OPG, Metrolinx Indigenous
      procurement pages (see `targets_and_contacts.md`) — *30 min*

> **Done when:** New matching tenders arrive in your inbox automatically from ≥3 sources.

---

## Week 6 — Apply to a federal supply arrangement

Standing offers / supply arrangements are where *recurring* call-up revenue lives.
Get in the pool now even though call-ups come later.

- [ ] **Read ProServices supply-arrangement requirements** (professional services,
      has an Indigenous supplier stream) — *45 min*
- [ ] **Check TBIPS** (Task-Based Informatics Professional Services) fit for your IT/AI work — *45 min*
- [ ] **Start the application** for whichever fits best (ProServices is usually the
      lower bar for a small AI firm) — *2 hrs*
- [ ] **Note the next intake/refresh window** in your calendar if applications are periodic — *15 min*

> **Done when:** One supply-arrangement application is submitted or queued for the next window.

      Innovation Initiative; they actively chase Indigenous-led applicants — *30 min to request*

      vertical #2. Realistic ask: $50K–$150K — *2.5 hrs*

> **Done when:** IRAP ITA conversation is booked and a draft project narrative exists.

## Week 8 — First warm outreach wave (8 contacts)

Now that credentials + capability statement exist, reach the people whose job is to find you.

- [ ] **Email Indigenous Procurement Coordinators** at 4 priority federal depts:
      ECCC, ISC, CIRNAC, DFO — attach capability statement, 1-paragraph intro — *2 hrs*
- [ ] **Email OFNTSC** (Ontario First Nations Technical Services Corp) — highest fit for
      Water-Intel; they advise First Nations on water infrastructure — *30 min*
- [ ] **Contact 1 crown-corp Indigenous procurement lead** (Hydro One or OPG) — *30 min*
- [ ] **Contact MCK Economic Development** (Kanesatake) + one larger Mohawk economic dev
      org (Tewatohnhi'saktha / Akwesasne) for the supplier-network angle — *45 min*
- [ ] **Log every send** in a simple tracker (date, contact, org, status, follow-up date) —
      reuse the format from `docs/AI_OUTREACH_PLAYBOOK.md` — *ongoing*

> **Done when:** 8 outreaches sent and logged with follow-up dates set.
