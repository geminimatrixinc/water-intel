## Tier 1 — Federal departments (5% target, AI/water fit)

Each has an **Indigenous Procurement Coordinator** — find via the dept's "doing business
with us" page or ask SAC-PAC@tpsgc-pwgsc.gc.ca.

| Dept | Why it fits | Priority |
|------|-------------|----------|
| **ECCC** (Environment & Climate Change) | Source water, environmental monitoring | ⭐ High |
| **ISC** (Indigenous Services Canada) | First Nations drinking water — named priority | ⭐ High |
| **CIRNAC** (Crown-Indigenous Relations) | Territory monitoring, IBA tracking | High |
| **DFO** (Fisheries & Oceans) | Water quality, aquatic monitoring | Med |
| **NRCan** (Natural Resources) | Environmental/territory data | Med |
| **Health Canada** | Water health, case-management AI | Med |
| **Shared Services Canada** | Large AI/ML/cloud budget | Med (broad IT) |
| **DND** | Phase 3 dept — green field for Indigenous suppliers | Opportunistic |

**Key federal contacts/inboxes:**
- Indigenous procurement general: indigenousprocurement@sac-isc.gc.ca · 1-800-400-7677
- Procurement support for business: SAC-PAC@tpsgc-pwgsc.gc.ca
- IBD team: REA-IBD@sac-isc.gc.ca
