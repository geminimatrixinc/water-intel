# Phase 1 — Foundation & Credentials (Weeks 1–4)

> **Goal:** By end of Week 4 you have current credentials, a started CCAB application,
> a reusable capability statement, and a repositioned company identity. This is the
> highest-ROI, lowest-hours phase. Nothing here requires winning anything yet.

**Status:** 🟢 Active
**Time budget:** ~8–12 hrs/week
**Momentum rule:** check at least one box every week.

---

## Week 1 — Lock down the federal credential (IBD)

The IBD listing is the single most important asset. A stale or narrow profile means
procurement officers searching the directory never find you.

- [ ] **Log in to the IBD** and pull Gemini Matrix's current profile
      (https://services.sac-isc.gc.ca/REA-IBD/) — *30 min*
- [ ] **Confirm eligibility data is current:** 51%+ Indigenous ownership, status proof on
      file, no suspension flags — *15 min*
- [ ] **Add/confirm the right NAICS codes** (see `targets_and_contacts.md` → NAICS list):
      541510, 541511, 518210, 541690, 541620 — *30 min*
- [ ] **Rewrite the capability summary** in the profile to explicitly say: AI/ML, agentic
      systems, MCP, water & environmental analytics, Python data pipelines — *45 min*
- [ ] If profile needs re-verification, **email REA-IBD@sac-isc.gc.ca** to start it
      (note: current backlog is long — start early) — *15 min*

> **Done when:** Gemini Matrix appears in an IBD public search under your AI/data NAICS
> codes with an AI-forward capability summary.

---

## Week 2 — Start the Ontario credential (CCAB) + entity hygiene

- [ ] **Read the CCAB Certified Aboriginal Business (CAB) requirements**
      (https://www.ccab.com/main/ccab_member/certification/) — *30 min*
- [ ] **Gather the CCAB document pack:** incorporation docs, shareholder register, proof
      of status, business registration — *1 hr*
- [ ] **Start the CCAB CAB application** (you can save and continue) — *1 hr*
- [ ] **Entity hygiene check:** confirm Gemini Matrix corporate filings, minute book, and
      shareholder register are current and consistent with 100% Indigenous ownership — *1 hr*
- [ ] **Create a `/credentials` folder** (local, secure — NOT in this repo) holding scanned
      copies of all the above so every future bid is copy-paste fast — *30 min*

> **Done when:** CCAB application is submitted (or saved at final-review stage) and all
> credential documents live in one organized place.

## Week 3 — Build the capability statement (your most-used artifact)

You'll attach this to every cold email, bid, and LinkedIn message. Build it once, well.

- [ ] **Draft the one-pager** using `capability-statement-gemini-matrix.md` as the source
      (created in `docs/knowledge-base/` — see note below) — *2 hrs*
- [ ] Sections: company snapshot · Indigenous ownership/certifications · core AI/ML
      capabilities · Water-Intel proof point · NAICS codes · differentiators (MCP/agents)
      · contact — *included above*
- [ ] **Export a clean PDF** with the Gemini Matrix brand — *45 min*
- [ ] **Write a 3-sentence "capability blurb"** (email-body version) you can paste fast — *20 min*
- [ ] **Update your LinkedIn headline + About** to "Indigenous-owned AI/ML systems
      integrator | Mohawk-owned | Gemini Matrix" — *30 min*

> **Note:** The capability-statement source doc is `docs/knowledge-base/capability-statement-gemini-matrix.md`
> (ask the assistant to generate it when you reach this week, or draft from the outline above).
> **Done when:** A polished one-page PDF + a paste-ready blurb both exist.
