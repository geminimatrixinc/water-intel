# Grants & Funding (Stackable)

> Non-dilutive money you can pursue as a 100% Indigenous-owned, part-time-founded
> Ontario corporation. Most of these are designed to **stack** — don't pick one.
> Water-Intel is your application vehicle.
>
> **Always verify current program limits and intake windows before applying** — amounts
> and rules change yearly.

---

## Tier 1 — Start now (you're directly eligible, no partner needed)

| Program | Body | Indicative $ | Why it fits |
|---------|------|-------------|-------------|
| **NRC IRAP — Indigenous Innovation Initiative** | NRC | ~$50K–$500K | Free ITA advisor, accepts part-time Indigenous-led founders, no partner |
| **Indigenous Economic Development Fund (IEDF)** | Ontario | Grant + loan envelopes | Ontario Indigenous-owned business — direct eligibility |
| **NACCA / Aboriginal Financial Institutions** | NACCA network | Patient capital, equity | Indigenous business financing |

**Action order:** IRAP first (free, parallel, costs nothing) → IEDF next (stacks).

## Stacking strategy

1. **IRAP** = anchor (free advisor, hardens the platform). Mention it in every other
   application — "in active discussions with NRC IRAP" raises credibility.
2. **IEDF** = Ontario layer, stacks on IRAP.
3. **Mitacs** = talent subsidy once a faculty partner signs (leverage existing McMaster
   outreach in `docs/AI_OUTREACH_PLAYBOOK.md`).
4. **ECCC / ISC water programs** = tie directly to a community pilot once one is warm.

> **Part-time reality:** apply to a maximum of **2 grants at a time.** A booked IRAP ITA
> call + one stacking application is plenty of throughput for 8–12 hrs/week. Quality of
> narrative beats quantity of applications.

---

## What every application needs (prep once, reuse)

- [ ] Capability statement PDF (from Phase 1)
- [ ] 1-paragraph + 1-page project narrative (Water-Intel → vertical #2)
- [ ] Proof of Indigenous ownership (status + shareholder register)
- [ ] Simple budget (your time, cloud/hosting, any contractor help)
- [ ] Outcome metrics (what the funding produces in 6–12 months)

> Keep these in your secure `/credentials` + `/grant-kit` folders (local, not in repo)
> so each new application is assembly, not authoring.
