## What this is

A **standing pre-qualification vehicle**, not a single contract. Get vetted once against mandatory criteria → sit in a pool → federal departments compete future AI task orders only among pool members. Running since 2018, already used by Health Canada, NRCan, IRCC, ESDC, ECCC, DFO, and others. Reissued periodically (this is at least the 3rd cycle) to admit new suppliers — evergreen, not one-shot.

This is likely the concrete instance of the "Set-Aside / IPP / sole-source" doors described in `planning/indigenous-track/00_strategy_overview.md` — worth cross-referencing once the ITQ document is in hand.

## Why it fits

- Category on the notice: **"Analytical or scientific software"** — matches Water-Intel/Gemini Matrix's actual work, not a stretch-fit like the NRC cleantech program
- No operational-deployment prerequisite (unlike NRC AI for Productivity Challenge — parked for that reason)
- No equity, no multi-month accelerator commitment (unlike VentureLab — parked for time-cost)
- Historical qualified-supplier list included small boutique AI shops and explicit **Joint Ventures** — not exclusively Accenture/IBM/Deloitte-scale
- The current notice has a **"Partner with another business"** matchmaking feature built in — teaming to meet criteria is an anticipated, supported pattern
