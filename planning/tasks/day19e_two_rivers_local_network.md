## Objective
Open the **local** relationship channel: Two Rivers Community Development Centre — the
Aboriginal-controlled economic development corporation serving Six Nations of the Grand River
and Mississaugas of the Credit. GREAT Building, 16 Sunrise Court, Ohsweken · 519-445-4567 ·
info@tworivers.ca · https://tworivers.ca

## Why This Matters
- **It's the closest node in the entire network** — minutes away, and their sole mandate is
  financing and supporting Indigenous-owned businesses in your service area ($44M+ loaned,
  800+ loans).
- **They are the IFI channel for non-repayable capital.** The Aboriginal Entrepreneurship
  Program (Access to Capital) flows through Indigenous Financial Institutions — up to ~$99K
  non-repayable for an entrepreneur. Asking them "does Gemini Matrix fit?" is a conversation,
  not an application.
- **Warm intros beat cold outreach.** Their workshops/events calendar and client network reach
  exactly the ec-dev and business community the outreach phases (Week 8, day19d) try to reach
  cold.
- **Free product discovery for grant-intel.** Their client base *is* grant-intel's target user.
  One meeting answers "would your clients use a tool that finds set-asides and funding?"

## Steps — 4 short sessions

### Session 1 — Prep (~30 min)
- [ ] Capability one-pager handy (same blurb as day19a Session 1)

      (+ grant-intel once its Day 16 ships — don't wait for it to book the meeting)
- [ ] Know the ask: an intro conversation — AEP fit, their programs, who else to meet.
      **Not a pitch.**

### Session 2 — Call first, then go (~15 min)
- [ ] **Call 519-445-4567** (verified 2026-07-15: no online booking; they're informal —
      "contact us today, get started tomorrow"). Script: *"I run an Indigenous-owned software
      business here in Ohsweken and I'd like to talk to someone about business financing and
      the AEP program — should I drop by, or is there a good time?"* Whatever they answer IS
      the appointment — you need a Business Development Officer, not the front desk
- [ ] Opportunistic alternative: if passing the GREAT Building (16 Sunrise Court) anyway, walk
      in, leave the one-pager, get the BDO's name — turns the follow-up call warm
- [ ] Email fallback if phone goes nowhere: info@tworivers.ca with the one-pager attached

### Session 3 — The meeting
- [ ] Ask: does Gemini Matrix fit the AEP Access to Capital stream (or their other financing)?
- [ ] Ask: workshops/events worth attending; other people/orgs they'd point a local
      Indigenous tech business to

      their clients would use it
- [ ] Take names — a Two Rivers relationship is also a future 19b-style community reference

### Session 4 — Log + follow through (~20 min)
