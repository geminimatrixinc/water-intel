## Why This Is First

Reference relationships, grants, and first revenue realistically take **~12 months.** These two
government levers do **not** — they need **no partner, no reference, no revenue, no community data.**
They are the things you can do *this month, alone*, while the slow Track B (19b–19d) bakes.

This is the answer to "I need to call the government to support AI." It's **NRC IRAP** (advisor +
R&D funding) and **SR&ED** (tax credits on AI development you're already doing). Not IFAC — that's
the international accounting body.

## Step A1 — NRC IRAP (the advisor + R&D funding)

**Status:** ❌ INELIGIBLE — requires 1 full-time T4 employee. Solo founder cannot apply.

The National Research Council's **Industrial Research Assistance Program** was considered but requires continuous full-time employment on payroll. Not an option until you hire a T4 employee (which happens after funding, not before).

**What NRC IRAP screener recommended instead:** Innovations Canada (ISED Innovative Solutions Canada) — likely better fit for early-stage, solo-founder stage.

## Step A1 (Revised) — Innovations Canada (the alternative government AI support)

**What it is:** ISED's Innovative Solutions Canada program — federal support for innovative solutions to government challenges.

> ⚠️ **Correction (2026-07-14):** ISC is **challenge-based procurement**, not an open grant. Federal
> departments post specific challenges; companies propose solutions to *those challenges*. You cannot
> apply with your own project on your own timeline. There are two streams: **Challenge Stream**
> (R&D funding against a posted challenge) and **Testing Stream** (government buys and tests
> pre-commercial prototypes — potentially the better fit for Water-Intel). The right posture is
> **monitor the challenge portal**, not "call to apply."

**Why it's still worth tracking:**
- No T4 employee requirement (solo founders / small businesses eligible)
- Non-dilutive; Testing Stream is effectively a first federal *customer*, which doubles as a reference
- Water/environmental-monitoring challenges do appear periodically

**Your next step (revised):**
- [ ] Check open challenges: https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/innovative-solutions-canada/en
- [ ] Verify current program status and both streams (program funding levels have shifted year to year — confirm what's active in 2026)
- [ ] Set a **monthly 15-min recurring check** of the portal for water / environmental / AI challenges
- [ ] Optional: one inquiry email asking whether environmental-monitoring challenges are expected in upcoming cycles

**Expected outcome:**
- Standing monitor in place; respond fast if a matching challenge posts
- Realistic expectations: this is opportunistic, not a pipeline you control

## Step A2 — SR&ED (the government pays you back for building AI)

The **Scientific Research & Experimental Development** tax incentive (CRA) refunds a share of R&D
costs where there's genuine **technological uncertainty** — which AI/ML development qualifies for.
For a Canadian-Controlled Private Corporation: **~35% federal refundable ITC + Ontario credits** on
eligible expenditures (salaries, contractor costs). It is **retroactive ~18 months**, so Water-Intel
work already done may be claimable.

> ⚠️ **Reality check (2026-07-14):** SR&ED refunds **expenditures actually incurred** — T4
> salaries, contractor invoices, eligible materials. **Unpaid founder labour is not claimable and
> dividends do not qualify.** If no salary or contractor costs have been paid for Water-Intel work,
> the retroactive claim is likely ~$0 no matter how strong the technical log is. The log is still
> worth keeping: it becomes valuable the moment you draw a T4 salary post-funding, and it doubles
> as evidence for grants and technical reviews. Confirm actual eligible expenditures with an
> accountant before budgeting any SR&ED money into the plan.

- [ ] Confirm Gemini Matrix is a CCPC (incorporation status) — required for the refundable rate
- [ ] Read the SR&ED overview: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/scientific-research-experimental-development-tax-incentive-program.html
- [ ] Start a lightweight **SR&ED log**: dates, technical problems, what was uncertain, what was tried, hours
  - This is the evidence base for a claim. Water-Intel's anomaly-model + pipeline work is the candidate project.
- [ ] Decide: self-file (T661 with the corporate return) vs. engage a SR&ED specialist (usually contingency-fee)
- [ ] Note the claim deadline relative to your fiscal year-end so retroactive eligibility isn't lost
