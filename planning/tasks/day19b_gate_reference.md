## Objective
Land **one** warm Indigenous-org relationship that produces a tangible output: a letter of support or a pilot/co-applicant commitment. A reference is what converts capability into Tier 1 procurement and into fundable grant applications.

- Mitacs and most grants need a partner letter that only a real relationship produces (see Step 3).
